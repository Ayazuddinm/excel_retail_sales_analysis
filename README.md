**Retail Sales Performance Dashboard**

An interactive Excel dashboard analysing a year of retail sales data. Built to practice the full analyst workflow: cleaning, pivot analysis, chart design, and turning numbers into a real business narrative.

**What's in here**

A 1,000-row retail sales dataset (Region, Category, Product, Units Sold, Revenue, Customer Satisfaction) turned into a dashboard with 4 KPIs, revenue breakdowns by Region/Category/Product, a monthly trend analysis, and 3 connected slicers that filter everything at once. Includes a written executive summary with findings and recommendations.

**Key findings**
South leads all regions, outperforming East by 24%, the widest gap in the dataset
Category performance is well balanced. Only a 14% spread top to bottom
Top 5 products are nearly tied (within 3.4%), then a real drop-off after that
May was the peak month, December the low, a 51% swing, though one year of data can't confirm seasonality
Tools used

Excel: PivotTables, Pivot charts, Slicers, structured Table references, dashboard design

**Process notes**

Built on live formulas, not hardcoded values, so it stays accurate if data grows. Caught and fixed real mistakes along the way, including a column overwrite and a misleading truncated chart axis.
